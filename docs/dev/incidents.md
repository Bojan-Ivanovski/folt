# Incident reports

An incident issue records a problem that was reported and its user impact. It is
not itself the fix. Create separate work items for investigation, mitigation,
or permanent correction, and link them from the incident.

Use the **Incident report** form. Keep the report factual and distinguish what
the reporter observed from what the team later learns.

## At report time

- Describe what the person was doing, saw, and expected; do not guess at a cause.
- Record who or what is affected, which workflow is disrupted, and the known
  scale or severity.
- Record reproduction steps and environment when available. Mark unknowns as
  unknown rather than filling gaps with assumptions.
- List workarounds and whether they have been verified.
- Attach or link useful evidence only after removing credentials, personal
  information, and unrelated private data.

## During investigation

Update the incident with material findings and the agreed response. Create
separate issues for implementation work and link each one. Keep the incident
focused on impact, mitigation, and status; do not duplicate the detailed plans
from the work issues.

## At resolution

Before closing, summarize what stopped or corrected the impact, link the issues
and pull requests involved, and identify the release or deployment when
applicable. If the cause remains unknown or follow-up work remains, say so and
link the tracking issue. Do not close solely because a workaround exists unless
the incident's impact is resolved and the remaining work is tracked.
